On September 23–24, 2026, the Accounting Department’s TwoMiles Team had the opportunity to learn from industry professionals and explore how Artificial Intelligence can be integrated into accounting and back-office operations.

As AI continues to transform the way businesses work, the sessions provided the team with valuable insights into how emerging technologies can support accounting professionals—not by replacing human expertise, but by helping teams work more efficiently, automate repetitive processes, and focus their time on tasks that require critical thinking and professional judgment.

Learning from Mr. Melvin Yuan

The team had the opportunity to meet Mr. Melvin Yuan, founder of Stellar, whose work focuses on building knowledgeable and autonomous AI agents and designing human-AI workflows.

Mr. Yuan shared his insights on the rapidly evolving relationship between people and AI, particularly on how businesses can adapt as AI becomes increasingly integrated into everyday operations. His discussion introduced the team to practical applications of AI in accounting processes, with a particular focus on tools such as Claude.

The session explored how AI can support accounting professionals in areas such as organizing information, assisting with documentation, streamlining repetitive tasks, analyzing data, and improving workflows. It also encouraged the team to think beyond simply asking AI to perform individual tasks and instead consider how AI can become part of a larger and more efficient workflow.

For the TwoMiles Team, this provided an opportunity to reflect on their existing accounting processes and identify areas where AI could potentially reduce manual work while allowing professionals to dedicate more time to analysis, decision-making, and client support.

Exploring U.S. Tax Filing with Juno

The team also had the chance to virtually meet Mr. Blend Suka, Account Executive at Juno, who introduced the team to Juno and its potential applications in U.S. tax filing.

The session gave the team an overview of how technology and AI-powered solutions can assist with tax-related workflows, particularly in making complex processes more organized and manageable. It also provided valuable exposure to tools designed for accounting professionals working with U.S. tax processes.

This discussion further demonstrated how AI can be applied beyond general productivity and into specialized areas of accounting and finance.

Building a More Efficient Human-AI Workflow

While the sessions covered different tools and applications, a common theme connected the discussions: AI can become a powerful partner in improving productivity when used effectively.

The team learned that maximizing AI is not simply about knowing which tool to use. It also involves understanding how to communicate with AI effectively, identifying the right tasks to delegate, reviewing AI-generated outputs, and maintaining human oversight throughout the process.

For accounting professionals, this balance is especially important. Accuracy, accountability, professional judgment, and confidentiality remain essential, while AI can help support the processes surrounding these responsibilities.

The sessions encouraged the TwoMiles Team to become more intentional in exploring AI—not only as a technology, but as a tool that can help create smarter and more efficient ways of working.

Looking Ahead

The Accounting Department’s exposure to these discussions marks another step toward embracing innovation in accounting and back-office operations. As AI continues to evolve, the TwoMiles Team looks forward to discovering more ways to integrate technology into their workflows while continuing to build on the knowledge, skills, and professional judgment that remain at the core of their work.

By combining accounting expertise with emerging AI capabilities, the team can continue exploring new possibilities for productivity, efficiency, and better ways of delivering quality work.
