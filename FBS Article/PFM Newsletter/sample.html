Luzon… Visayas… Mindanao… Here we come!

Recently, our team conducted a church-planting training in Naval, Leyte—a long yet deeply rewarding journey. From day one, the pastors and workers were so challenged and impressed, many were asking us to bring the training to their churches and communities. Even the region’s largest church organization has now invited us to share our training with them.

On the final day, participants were asked to write down their big dreams for their churches and cities. Watching Pastors and leaders present and discuss their dreams, many in tears, is profoundly moving. The emotion in the room was palpable as people cried out to God, seeking His guidance to grow and establish more churches. What stood out the most was seeing both young and old pastors reignite their passion for the mission of Christ. Their sincerity and renewed sense of calling deeply touched our hearts.

A particularly emotional moment came when two individuals with disabilities shared their dream to actively advance God’s Kingdom. Despite their challenges, they expressed such a bold faith and determination. Their courage to pursue their calling, despite their circumstances, was incredibly inspiring everyone. It was a powerful reminder that God’s calling knows no boundaries.

We believe this is just the beginning, and are excited to see God continue to transform churches and communities across the Visayan Islands (central Philippines).

To all who pray, support, and participate with us in other ways—thank you! Fires are being lit, and we know these will continue to burn for His glory!
