Celebrating a Decade of God’s Faithfulness and Looking Ahead

This quarter, we celebrated the 10th Anniversary of Frontline Worship Center (FWC) Atisan, our church in the mountain, while also honoring Ptr. Rolly’s 14th year in ministry. What began as a simple vision to make disciples and plant house churches has grown into a thriving network of believers committed to advancing God’s Kingdom. Through every season, God has remained faithful.

One of the highlights of the celebration was gathering leaders from across our house church network in San Pablo City and neighboring communities. Their fellowship and testimonies reflected the fruit of years of disciple-making and leadership development. It was especially encouraging to see disciples becoming leaders who are now raising up others to expand the reach of the Gospel.

This quarter also marked the commissioning of our FWC San Isidro leaders, another exciting step as God continues opening new doors for ministry and the multiplication of disciples and house churches.

Our anniversary theme, “Made for More,” reminds us that God continues to call us to greater Kingdom impact. As we celebrate His faithfulness over the past decade, we look ahead with confidence, trusting Him to lead us into the next chapter of His mission.
