We are proud to announce a new Campus Learning Partnership between Frontline Business Solutions (FBS) and Laguna University, in collaboration with the Laguna University – Accounting Information Society (LU-AISSoc).

This partnership began with LU-AISSoc’s initiative to collaborate with FBS for its training and seminar programs, recognizing the importance of equipping students with practical skills that extend beyond the classroom. It marks another step toward bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world industry experience.

Bringing Industry Expertise into the Classroom

As part of this partnership, FBS will serve as the resource speaker for Laguna University’s QuickBooks Online Webinar. The webinar is designed to introduce students to one of the most widely used cloud accounting platforms in today’s business environment, giving future accounting and business professionals valuable exposure to the tools they are likely to encounter in the workplace.

By connecting students with industry practitioners, the program provides insights that complement academic knowledge and prepares them for the demands of their future careers.

Why Campus Learning Partnerships Matter

At FBS Learning Solutions, we believe that education becomes more impactful when schools and industry work together. Our mission is to help students and professionals upskill, build practical competencies, and become industry-ready through relevant training, seminars, and hands-on learning experiences.

Through partnerships like this, we aim to empower learners with in-demand digital and professional skills while supporting educational institutions in delivering meaningful career development opportunities.
