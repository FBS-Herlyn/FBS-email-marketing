FBS conducted an Employee Development Program today, bringing together employees from FBS and affiliated companies and organizations under the Philippine Frontline Ministries and Frontline Group of Companies for a day of learning, collaboration, and professional growth.

The program featured two seminars that focused on important workplace competencies: “The Ideal Team Player” and “Effective Workload Management.” Through these sessions, participants were given practical knowledge and strategies that they can apply in their day-to-day work.

Building the Ideal Team Player

The first seminar, The Ideal Team Player, focused on three essential qualities that contribute to becoming an effective and valuable member of a team: Humble, Hungry, and Smart.

Participants learned how humility helps team members recognize the contributions of others, remain open to learning, and work collaboratively without seeking personal recognition. Being Hungry means having the initiative, drive, and willingness to go beyond the minimum in fulfilling responsibilities. Meanwhile, being Smart highlights the importance of using good judgment and emotional intelligence when communicating and working with others.

Together, these three qualities provide a practical framework for understanding what makes a strong team player. The session encouraged participants to reflect on these characteristics and how they can apply them in their own roles and relationships within the workplace.

Managing Workloads More Effectively

The second seminar, Effective Workload Management, introduced participants to the Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4 framework as a way of understanding and managing different types of tasks based on their level of urgency and importance.

Participants explored how responsibilities can be categorized into four quadrants, helping them identify which tasks require immediate attention, which should be scheduled and planned, which may be delegated when appropriate, and which can be minimized or avoided.

The session emphasized that effective workload management is not simply about completing more tasks, but about knowing what to prioritize, where to focus time and energy, and how to manage responsibilities effectively. By understanding the different quadrants, participants gained a practical approach to organizing their workload and making more intentional decisions about their daily tasks.

Led by an Experienced Facilitator

The seminars were facilitated by Mr. Jhonny Jose Dichoso, who served as the speaker and facilitator for the Employee Development Program.

Through interactive discussions and practical workplace applications, Mr. Dichoso guided the participants in understanding how the topics can be applied to their respective roles and organizations.

His facilitation provided participants with opportunities to reflect on their current work practices and consider ways to become more effective contributors within their teams.

From FBS Employees to a Broader Learning Community

Employee development seminars have been an annual initiative at FBS, traditionally conducted as part of the development and onboarding of new employees.

This year, FBS expanded the initiative by opening the program to employees from other affiliated companies and organizations under the Philippine Frontline Ministries. This expansion allowed participants from different organizations to learn together, exchange perspectives, and build stronger connections across the affiliated community.

By making the program more inclusive, FBS aims to contribute to a culture where continuous learning and professional development are shared not only within the company but also among its partner organizations.

As FBS continues to conduct development initiatives, the company looks forward to creating more opportunities where employees and affiliated organizations can learn together, grow together, and serve their respective teams and communities more effectively.
