FBS participated in the 2nd PCCI San Pablo City Job Fair 2026 held on September 12, 2026, at the SM City San Pablo Atrium, bringing together jobseekers, employers, and members of the local business community for a day centered on employment, career opportunities, and business connections.

Organized by the Philippine Chamber of Commerce and Industry – San Pablo City (PCCI-SPC) in partnership with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) and the Public Employment Service Office (PESO), the job fair provided an accessible venue for jobseekers to connect directly with companies offering employment opportunities. The event was also held in celebration of Civil Service Month.

For FBS, the event served not only as an opportunity to meet potential members of the FBS team but also as a platform to strengthen relationships within the local business community.

FBS Welcomes More Than 130 Applicants

FBS participated in the job fair with opportunities for Accounting Associate and Virtual Assistant positions.

Throughout the event, the FBS booth welcomed more than 130 applicants interested in exploring career opportunities with the company. Applicants had the opportunity to learn more about FBS, its work environment, and the roles available across its growing business operations.

Representing FBS on-site were members of the HR Team, Marketing Team, Web Specialist, and Accounting Specialist, who worked together throughout the event to accommodate applicants, introduce the company, and support the recruitment process.

The participation of different teams also allowed applicants to gain a broader perspective of the opportunities available within FBS and the different functions that contribute to delivering business solutions to clients.

Beyond Recruitment: Strengthening Business Connections

While recruitment was a major focus of FBS' participation, the job fair also created opportunities to engage with the wider business community.

During the event, the FBS team had the chance to connect and reconnect with fellow companies, opening conversations with potential clients while strengthening relationships with existing clients.

These interactions provided an opportunity for FBS to introduce its range of business solutions and learn more about the needs and challenges of other organizations.

As a company providing IT, Virtual Assistance, Accounting, and Learning Solutions, FBS recognizes the value of building strong relationships with businesses within the community. Events such as the PCCI-SPC Job Fair provide an avenue not only for talent acquisition but also for networking, collaboration, and identifying opportunities where FBS can support businesses through its services.

FBS: Creating Opportunities Through People and Partnerships

For FBS, being part of the 2nd PCCI San Pablo City Job Fair represents more than participation in a recruitment event.

It reflects FBS' commitment to creating opportunities for local talent, strengthening relationships within the business community, and continuously growing as a solutions provider for businesses locally and internationally.

The event allowed FBS to meet potential candidates, engage with fellow businesses, reconnect with current partners, and explore new opportunities for collaboration.

FBS extends its appreciation to PCCI San Pablo City, DOLE, PESO San Pablo City, the City Government of San Pablo, the participating companies, and all the jobseekers who contributed to the success of the event.

We look forward to continuing to build meaningful connections, create opportunities, and contribute to a stronger and more connected business community.

More Talent. More Connections. More Opportunities.
