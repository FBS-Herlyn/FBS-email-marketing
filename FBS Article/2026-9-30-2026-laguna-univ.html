Accounting students today are preparing for a profession that goes beyond understanding accounting concepts on paper. As businesses increasingly rely on digital tools for bookkeeping, reporting, and financial management, gaining practical experience with accounting software has become an important part of becoming career-ready.

To help bridge classroom learning and real-world accounting practice, FBS was invited by the Laguna University Accounting Information System Society (LU-AISSoc) to conduct a QuickBooks Online Webinar for BSAIS students of Laguna University.

The webinar featured Ms. Thea Lyzette Consignado of FBS as the resource speaker, who guided the students through the fundamentals and practical application of QuickBooks Online.

Connecting Classroom Learning to Accounting Careers

The webinar began with a Career Connection segment, designed to help students understand how their accounting and information systems knowledge can translate into actual workplace skills and career opportunities.

The discussion highlighted how familiarity with cloud-based accounting platforms such as QuickBooks Online can complement their academic background and prepare them for opportunities in bookkeeping, accounting support, virtual assistance, and other finance-related roles.

Understanding QuickBooks Online and Modern Accounting

Ms. Consignado introduced the students to QuickBooks Online and its role in modern accounting.

The session connected familiar accounting concepts with the way transactions are recorded, organized, and processed within a digital accounting system. This allowed students to see how concepts learned in their BSAIS subjects can be applied in an actual accounting environment.

The students then participated in a QuickBooks Online Tour, where Ms. Consignado walked them through the platform's interface, navigation, and key features.

The guided tour gave participants an overview of where different accounting activities are performed and how information moves throughout the system before proceeding to the hands-on exercises.

Putting Accounting Concepts Into Practice

A significant portion of the webinar focused on Guided Hands-on Training, allowing students to actively perform accounting activities in QuickBooks Online.

Session 1 — Business Setup and Revenue Cycle

The first hands-on session focused on setting up a business and processing the revenue cycle step by step.

Under Ms. Consignado's guidance, students worked through practical activities that demonstrated how business information can be established in QuickBooks Online and how revenue-related transactions can be recorded and managed.

Session 2 — Expenditure Cycle, Banking, and Reporting

The second session covered the expenditure cycle, banking, and reporting.

Students explored how expenditure-related transactions are recorded, how banking activities are incorporated into the accounting process, and how accounting data can be used to generate reports.

These exercises allowed participants to see how individual transactions contribute to the overall accounting cycle and eventually affect financial information and reports.

Analyzing Real-World Transactions

To apply what they learned, students participated in an "Analyze: Where Should We Record These Transactions?" activity.

Rather than simply following instructions, participants were presented with real-world transaction scenarios and encouraged to determine how and where these transactions should be recorded in QuickBooks Online.

The activity emphasized that effective use of accounting software requires more than knowing the technical steps. A strong understanding of the underlying accounting concepts remains essential when making decisions within a digital accounting system.

Preparing Future Accounting Professionals

The QuickBooks Online Webinar provided BSAIS students with an opportunity to connect their academic knowledge with practical accounting technology.

Through the collaboration between LU-AISSoc and FBS, students were able to explore a widely used cloud-based accounting platform while gaining hands-on experience with business setup, revenue and expenditure cycles, banking, reporting, and transaction analysis.

With Ms. Thea Lyzette Consignado serving as the resource speaker, the session aimed to help students take their first steps toward becoming more confident and digitally equipped accounting professionals.

The webinar served as a meaningful bridge between classroom concepts and real-world accounting practice, reinforcing the importance of combining accounting knowledge with technology as students prepare for their future careers.
