At FBS, we believe that success is more meaningful when it creates a positive impact beyond the workplace. As part of our mission to positively impact the communities in which we exist , we strive not only to provide excellent service to our clients but also to give back and create opportunities for others.

One of the ways we live out this mission is through our Financial Sponsorship Program , a partnership between FBS and Frontline Christian Academy (FCA) .

Creating Opportunities Through Education

Through the program, FCA identifies and screens students within the community who demonstrate potential and a strong desire to learn but may need financial support to pursue their education.

Each year, the selected 10 students receive support through the FBS Financial Sponsorship Program. Their progress is evaluated annually based on the program’s academic requirements, ensuring that the scholarship continues to reach students who are committed to their education. As scholars complete the program or as opportunities open up, new students may be welcomed into the program—allowing FBS to continue supporting 10 scholars at a time.

For these scholars, the sponsorship goes beyond simply covering tuition. FBS supports their essential school needs, including:

  • Tuition and miscellaneous fees
  • Books and learning materials
  • School supplies
  • School uniforms
  • Other necessary educational expenses

The goal is simple: to remove financial barriers so students can focus on what truly matters—learning, growing, and building their future.

Students who consistently meet the program's expectations are given the opportunity to continue receiving support until they complete Junior High School.
