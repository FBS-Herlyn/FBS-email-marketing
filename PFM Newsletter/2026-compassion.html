Hello, Friends and Ministry Partners,

Psalm 121:8
“The Lord will keep your going out and your coming in from this time forth and forevermore.”

It seems that whenever I write these updates, we are either “going out” or “coming in” to the Philippines. By the time you receive this, we will be back in Canada. God has faithfully kept us through these many years. Travelers sometimes lose luggage, but in 15 years of going back and forth, we have never lost a single bag. Small as it may seem, it is another reminder of God’s faithfulness.

We recently attended the Frontline Christian Academy (FCA) Recognition and Graduation ceremonies, where I had the privilege of walking two of our children onstage. One was Celine, who graduated Grade 10. I realized I was there for her “coming in” years ago and am now here for her “going out” as she begins senior high school.

The other was Minos, who received the Fortitude Award. Afterwards, we talked about what fortitude means—the courage to face life’s difficulties. His own “coming in” required that courage as he learned to trust, to be cared for instead of harmed, and to live in community. God continues His good work in him.

Our newest child is six-year-old Remelyn. When she first arrived, she could barely lift her head to make eye contact because of the abuse she had suffered. Today, she eagerly talks about everything she sees and experiences. Safety has allowed the resilience God placed within her to begin restoring her.

We also had the joy of hosting the Philippine Frontline Ministries U.S. Board. FTC leadership shared both our progress and challenges, and together we spent time with the children, shared a meal, worshiped around a bonfire, and were encouraged by God's Word.

God willing, Connie and I will continue our “going out” and “coming in” for years to come. Please keep our health in your prayers. Thank you for your continued friendship and partnership. We pray that you, too, will know God’s faithful care as you “go out” and “come in.”

Blessings,
Michael
