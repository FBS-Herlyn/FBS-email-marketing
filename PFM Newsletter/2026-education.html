Dear friends and partners,

As we prepare for another school year at Frontline Christian Academy (FCA), our hearts are filled with gratitude for all God has done. This has been a season of growth—not only in enrollment, but in opportunities to impact more children and families for Christ.

As of this writing, enrollment has reached over 300 students, our highest ever. We praise God for every family He has brought to FCA and pray for continued growth in the years ahead. Construction of our Senior High School building is also progressing well and will allow us to serve even more students in the future. We are trusting God for continued provision to provide the $200,000 US needed for the next phase.

God has blessed our school in many practical ways this year. We were able to provide a salary increase for our employees, welcome additional staff to better support our teachers, equip every teacher with a new laptop, and improve campus internet connectivity. These investments allow our faculty to focus more fully on teaching, mentoring, and discipling students.

We are also excited to expand our Sports Program, launch our new Homeschool Program, and prepare for the implementation of the Dynamic Learning Program (DLP)—a proven approach that develops independent, critical-thinking learners while strengthening academic achievement. We are especially thankful that most of our faculty are now Licensed Professional Teachers, reflecting our commitment to excellence in Christian education.

One of our greatest blessings continues to be the faithfulness of our people. God has blessed us with consistently high teacher and student retention, and FCA's reputation continues to grow throughout San Pablo City and neighboring communities.

As you pray for FCA, please pray for wisdom for our leadership and staff, God's continued provision for the Senior High School project and future developments, and continued growth in enrollment. Above all, pray that FCA will continue to develop young people who grow in wisdom, character, and faith, bringing glory to God in every area of life.

Thank you for partnering with us as we invest in the next generation. If you are interested in partnering financially or would like additional information, please write me at rhoda.pessina@fca.edu.ph — I would love to hear from you.
