Webinar Description

This webinar is designed to introduce participants to the fundamentals of accounting and bookkeeping, focusing on the basic principles used in recording and analyzing business transactions. Participants will learn essential accounting concepts such as debit and credit, chart of accounts, journal entries, and the accounting cycle.

The webinar will also provide an introduction to QuickBooks Online, allowing participants to explore its core functionalities through a live walkthrough using the QuickBooks Test Drive platform. This session aims to help aspiring Virtual Assistants, beginner bookkeepers, and business support professionals understand how accounting concepts are applied in modern digital bookkeeping systems.
